Key Insights & Recommendations
Actionable findings from the data
📈 Capture the seasonal surge
Revenue grew 80% from August to November 2011. Launch Q3 marketing campaigns by August and stock up top SKUs in September to fully capture the holiday gifting window.
🌍 International expansion opportunity
Netherlands, Germany, and France show strong demand. Localising the shopping experience (currency, language, shipping) in these markets could grow international share from 18% to 25%+.
🏆 Protect flagship products
Top 10 products drive 11%+ of revenue. Zero stockouts should be the rule for Paper Craft Little Birdie and Regency Cakestand — these are the business's highest-value SKUs.
👤 Convert anonymous buyers
25% of raw transactions lacked a Customer ID. A checkout registration incentive (discount, loyalty points) could convert these into trackable, retargetable customers.
