Top recommendations
Re-target prior successes first — 978 of 1,511 converted (64.7%). This is a ready-made warm list.
Shift budget from May to Mar/Sep/Oct/Dec — May burns 30% of contacts for only 6.7% conversion. Peak months hit 43–52%.
Classify unknown contacts — 13,020 leads languishing at 4.1% conversion. Cellular delivers 14.9%.
Cap outreach at 3 calls — conversion rate falls from 14.6% (call 1) to 9.0% (call 4) and keeps declining. After 3 contacts, cut losses.
Prioritize students & retirees — 28.7% and 22.8% conversion vs 7.3% blue-collar. Segment your dialing lists.
